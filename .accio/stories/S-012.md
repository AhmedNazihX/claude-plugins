## Context
After this ships: a build in a new or freshly set-up repo can't silently skip its tests, a rebuilt story never hands back its old unreviewed work as done, and a direct fix to accio gets accio's own reviewers. We'd know because the next iteration has no stop caused by any of these three, and a direct fix's review comes from `accio-brief --review` instead of a hand-written package.

This story is design "Lessons from iteration 1", part 3 (R3). On a rebuild the builder found its old commit on `story/S-001`, saw its tests pass, and handed it back unchanged.
## Reuse
The existing brief builder and template.
## Definition of Done
- [ ] S-012/1 WHEN `story/<ID>` has commits past its recorded base THE SYSTEM SHALL add a "## Your earlier work" section to the brief listing those commits
- [ ] S-012/2 WHEN that section is written THE SYSTEM SHALL say the commits were not accepted
- [ ] S-012/3 WHEN the story has review notes or an answered question THE SYSTEM SHALL show them beside that section as the reason
- [ ] S-012/4 WHEN the story branch has no commits past its base THE SYSTEM SHALL leave the section out
- [ ] S-012/5 (no test) checks pass: `cd accio && python3 -m unittest discover -s tests`
