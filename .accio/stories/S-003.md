## Context
After this ships: the user can use accio as their everyday coding workflow and finds it adds value, and we'd know because they use it themselves and learn from what goes wrong. For howler: after owl pushes, without being asked, a short report appears in the chat within a few minutes of CI ending. It gives PASSED or FAILED for each workflow and job; for a failure, the failing step or test and its error, `real` or `first failure`, and whether the cause lies inside the pushed range. We'd know by running one real iteration in this repo (howler itself), pushing, and seeing the report arrive while the user does something else.

This story is part 3 of the design (R4). It waits for the spike S-004 (the by-hand spike S-029/0): its results must be in S-004's Review notes before this story starts, and if its step 1 failed this story does not start.

`test_owl_skill.py` is a new text check on `accio/skills/owl/SKILL.md`. It proves what the skill says, not that a model follows it; /7 covers the model.
