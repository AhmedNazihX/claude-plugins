## Context
After this ships:
- mad-eye replies with the DoD ids it `checked` and `skipped`, and brew leaves a note when it checked none, instead of
  counting empty findings as a pass.
- `/accio` stops when a step ends with an `accio-…:` error, a refusal, or a "What I need from you" that isn't
  "nothing", and says that step's message, instead of reading the board and running the same step again.
We'd know because a stub mad-eye that checks no line gives a note, and the conductor's eval stops on prophecy's
"no checks" refusal.

Why: the gap S-076 and S-080 closed for boggart and the code reviewers is still open for mad-eye: its reply schema
(brew.js) has `findings` only, so a page that never loaded, a failed login or every line skipped looks clean. And
`skills/accio/SKILL.md` says "do the step, then go back to 1" with no stop rule: a step that refuses while the board
still names it runs again (S-077 was this loop for `sort`). wand's helpers are S-090.

Design: (1) brew.js mad-eye schema: `checked` and `skipped` (arrays of DoD ids) required; `checked` empty → the
story goes on with the note "mad-eye checked no DoD line (<skipped ids or its error>)"; mad-eye.md says what to put
in each. (2) accio skill: after a step's skill ends, stop and pass on its message when it ended with an error, a
refusal or a "What I need from you" other than "nothing" / "next: …"; only then read the board again. (3) Tests
and a conductor eval.
