## Context
After this ships: `/accio` goes from a new design note straight to sort, every flag that decides whether a review step runs comes from a script, and mad-eye can't quietly wander off the preview. We'd know because after the next wand note `accio-board next` says `sort`; `brew.js` asks portkey to compute no flag itself; a test shows an off-origin page after a click is hidden from mad-eye and fenced; auror's checklist has the two-parsers line; and the next story that adds an agent lists `accio/README.md` in its `files`.

Why this story: requirement L2 of "Group B leftovers". S-047's builder warned that `ui`, `preview` and `test_data` reach `brew.js` through portkey (Haiku) matching globs by hand, the weak spot S-058 fixed for `code` and `breaker`.

Design: `review._diff_facts` also returns `ui` (a changed path matches `ui_globs`, with `globs.matches_any`, as `risky_files` does), `preview` (config has a `preview` mapping) and `test_data` (that mapping has `"test_data": true`). `PACKAGE_SCHEMA.required` becomes `code, breaker, ui, preview, test_data`, and `packageFor` only asks portkey to copy the flags as given.
## Reuse
`review._diff_facts`, `risky_files` and `globs.matches_any` in `accio/bin/accio_lib/`; `PACKAGE_SCHEMA` and `packageFor` in `accio/workflows/brew.js`; stub agents in `accio/tests/workflow/stub.mjs`.
## Definition of Done
- [ ] S-061/1 WHEN a diff touches a path matching `ui_globs` THE SYSTEM SHALL give `ui: true`, and `ui: false` when none matches
- [ ] S-061/2 WHEN config has a `preview` mapping THE SYSTEM SHALL give `preview: true`, and false when it has none
- [ ] S-061/3 WHEN the `preview` mapping has `"test_data": true` THE SYSTEM SHALL give `test_data: true`, and false otherwise
- [ ] S-061/4 WHEN the package step's reply lacks `ui`, `preview` or `test_data` THE SYSTEM SHALL retry it as a failed reply
- [ ] S-061/5 WHEN `brew.js` is read THE SYSTEM SHALL hold no instruction for portkey to match globs or compute a flag
- [ ] S-061/6 (no test) checks pass: `python3 tests/run_tests.py` and `node --test 'tests/workflow/**/*.test.mjs'`
