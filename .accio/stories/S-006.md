## Context
After this ships: the user types one command (`/accio`) and accio carries the work from wherever the board stands to a pushed, CI-checked result, stopping only at real decisions. We'd know because in one real iteration the transcript has a single `/accio`, every other message from the user answers a real question, and no step ends with "type /accio:X".

This story is the conductor (design "One command: /accio", part 3, decision 003). It runs `accio-board next --json`, runs that step's skill in the same chat, and repeats while `auto` is true. Python decides continue or stop; the skill only follows it. The by-hand spikes (part 2: S1 and the wake) are story S-014 and are done first. Read the last two lines of its Review notes: they decide whether the skill starts the build itself or says "type /accio:brew", and whether it picks up at review after the build.
## Reuse
`accio-board next` from S-005. Other skills' layout under `accio/skills/`. The spike results in S-014's Review notes.
