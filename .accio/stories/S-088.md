## Context
After this ships: sort gives quill the design headings it hasn't sorted yet (from `run/unsorted.json`) and the board's
story list (ID, title, status, one line each, from `accio-board board --print`), and quill reads only those design
sections and the story files it needs for a dependency, instead of the whole `design.md` and every story. We'd know
because sort's prompt to quill names the headings and carries the story list, and quill's prompt never says to read
the whole design or every story.

Why: `agents/quill.md` says "Read `.accio/design.md`. Glob `.accio/stories/S-*.md` and read each story already
there." In this repo that is 61 KB of design and 233 KB of stories, about 75k tokens per sort run, and the design is
over the long-document guard's 40 KB limit (`hooks/doc_size.py`), so quill's first Read is blocked outright. The
unsorted headings are already known: `accio-board record-design` writes them to `run/unsorted.json`, and the board
lists every story.

Design: (1) sort step 2: read `run/unsorted.json` (the headings) and run `accio-board board --print`; pass both to
quill in the Write prompt; with no unsorted headings (a first design), pass "the whole design" and quill reads it
section by section (the guard allows a Read with an offset and limit). (2) quill.md Write job: find each named
heading with Grep, Read only that section (offset and limit), take existing stories from the list, and read a story
file only to depend on it. (3) A sort eval: a big design with one new note heading; quill reads only that section.
