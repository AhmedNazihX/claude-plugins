## Context
After this ships: `/accio` goes from a new design note straight to sort, every flag that decides whether a review step runs comes from a script, and mad-eye can't quietly wander off the preview. We'd know because after the next wand note `accio-board next` says `sort`; `brew.js` asks portkey to compute no flag itself; a test shows an off-origin page after a click is hidden from mad-eye and fenced; auror's checklist has the two-parsers line; and the next story that adds an agent lists `accio/README.md` in its `files`.

Why this story: requirement L1 of "Group B leftovers". Two design notes in a row left `accio-board next` saying `done`, so sort was run by hand. `nextstep.py` says sort only with zero stories.

Design: `record-design` compares the `## ` headings of the new `design.md` with the committed one (`git show HEAD:.accio/design.md`; none committed means every heading is new) and writes the new ones to `run/unsorted.json`. `accio-board record` (sort's step 5) empties it, also when no story was added. `next_step` says `sort` while the file lists a heading, checked before the `backlog → prophecy` rule. A missing or unreadable file means nothing is waiting.
## Reuse
`accio/bin/accio_lib/nextstep.py` `next_step`, the `record-design` command in `accio/bin/accio-board`.
