## Context
After this ships: a review answer or reject reason can't send a builder into a file its story may not touch without the session seeing it first, so no build round is lost to an "out of bounds" check the answer itself caused.

Why: in Jaza on 2026-10-24 (iteration 30), the main session answered S-088 with "add accessors to PaneTerminalSession", but S-088's `files:` held only `App/JazaTests/`, CI and docs. The builder followed the answer, prefect failed it three rounds as out of bounds (the headmaster ruled the builder right, but nothing could widen the story mid-run), and the story ended blocked again: a whole round lost. The same day S-058 was stopped for files it needed but its story didn't list. The author asked for an accio improvement (2026-10-24); they chose to catch it at review, where a person sees it, over letting the headmaster widen bounds during an unattended build.

What to build: `accio-owl check` and `close` look for repo paths in each `--answer` and `--reject` text (backticked or bare, with a `/` or a known source extension, relative to the repo root; a path that doesn't exist yet counts if its folder does) and compare each with that story's `files:` the way prefect's out-of-bounds check does (a folder entry covers what is under it). A path outside them makes `check` refuse, naming the story and the paths, and suggesting `--widen <ID> <path>`. New flag `--widen <ID> <path>` (repeatable): `check` accepts it; `close` adds the path to the story's `files:` (keeping the line's comment, line endings and mode, like `storyfiles.with_fields`) and commits it with the review. `--widen` for a story that is accepted, dropped or not in the iteration is refused. The owl skill's step 3 says to answer such a refusal with `--widen` (or by rewording the answer), and that widening is shown to the user in the summary line.
## Definition of Done
- [ ] S-075/1 WHEN an `--answer` or `--reject` text names a repo path outside that story's `files:` THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse `accio-owl check`, naming the story and each such path, and suggest `--widen`
- [ ] S-075/2 WHEN the path is covered by a folder entry in `files:`, or the text names no path THE SYSTEM SHALL accept the answer as today
- [ ] S-075/3 WHEN `--widen <ID> <path>` is given THE SYSTEM SHALL accept that path in `check`, and `close` SHALL add it to the story's `files:` and include the story file in the review commit
- [ ] S-075/4 WHEN `--widen` names a story that is accepted, dropped or not in the reviewed iteration THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse it
- [ ] S-075/5 WHEN the owl skill reports a review that widened a story THE SYSTEM SHALL say which story got which path in the "What happened" line
- [ ] S-075/6 (no test) checks pass: `python3 tests/run_tests.py` and `node --test 'tests/workflow/**/*.test.mjs'`
